Each line of the spec is marked **Done**, **Partial** (built in a simpler form) or **Not done**.
Circuit benchmark numbers are filled in from `--view=bench --rx` runs (3 laps, solo and with a grid).

## Track polish pass

- Ground now conforms to the road surface (banking, wide bowls, Joker lane) with embankments; the audit (`--view=bench --rx --rxcheck`) finds no road buried under terrain. Terrain poking through the road (shapeless wedges) is fixed.
- Where two roads at different heights are close, the ground takes the lower one; bridge spans are sized to the crossing angle; support piers go under any stretch standing more than 1.8 m clear.
- Large props (containers, campervans, paddock, tower, bleachers, floodlights, arch columns) are solid and kept clear of every road and lane; mining relics became stone block piles.
- Wall contact no longer bleeds all speed when a car leans on Armco.
- Remaining: gaps of 1-3 m between road and ground at a few embankment starts, and two stray scenery pieces on Timberline's lane side.

## Second polish pass

- Crossing decks follow the road's height and banking (no step at the ends) and sit on concrete piers.
- Start gantry rebuilt as a braced steel truss with footings; the duplicate checkered arch is no longer drawn on circuits.
- The last sample on a closed circuit had a reversed direction, which made the closing wall piece cross the whole road at the lap line. Fixed for every circuit.
- The Joker lane's outer wall now begins at its true position instead of jumping sideways at the entrance.
- Wall contact: no per-step speed drain, no bounce or spin from leaning contacts, and a car pinned against a wall is eased away so it can always drive off.
- Lap gates tolerate the road margin, so a bridge deck can no longer hide a lap crossing.
- Loose-surface drag and lap-1 wetness reduced again; Fjord's steepest climb softened from 24% to about 15%.

## Third polish pass

- Paddock awning was placed at the world origin (floating over the start line on several circuits); now in its own frame on posts.
- Apex bollards stand on the road at their own station; trackside props stand on the conformed ground.
- Joker lane opens as a wedge instead of a squared-off mouth; each lane has one breakable hay-bale chicane (adds roughly 1-2 s).
- Joker status tag on the HUD ("JOKER: TO DO / NEEDED NOW / DONE").
- Every scenery piece is rejected if it would stand on any road section or Joker lane; buildings keep a 2.5 m extra gap.
- Forest dirt grip raised and drag trimmed (standing start to 80 km/h about 3.0-3.4 s, was 4.0 s).

## Fourth polish pass

- Breton Dustbowl: shallow water splash across the road and a table jump added.
- Bauxite Cauldron: profile flattened (the lap used to end on a crest and dive about 10% past the line); steepest grade now about 9%.
- Joker lanes roughly twice as long (capped at 300 m; Granite about 1.2x, the only stretch without opposing bends), moved clear of crossings, jumps, tunnels and the start line. Entry and exit are blended over 36-80 m, with the island appearing after the lane has opened.
- Island walls only stand where the road-side and lane-side walls clear each other (they used to cross and squeeze cars).
- Outer walls now pull a car back in if it gets beyond them (tunnelling at lane exits).
- Cockpit and HUD speeds share one scale.
- Terrain claim allows for sample spread on bends, so no ground shows through lanes on hairpins.
- Benchmark: all 12 AI cars take the Joker; measured cost +1.3 to +4.5 s.

## Fifth polish pass

- Semi-transparent smoke and dust; 3D rumble strips (raised domed blocks with end faces).
- Jumps about 1.9x higher with a shorter, steeper take-off ramp and a higher launch limit on circuits (flights about 13-15 m).
- Car ground height eases from road height to terrain over 3 m past the edge (no sinking or bouncing when a wheel clips the edge).
- Bank ramps roll in over a longer distance; Funnel Ring's banked hairpin softened.
- Two-tier ground fit: a lower road's shoulder no longer overrules a higher road's own edge.
- Joker hay chicanes stand on the straightest stretch of the lane and the AI threads the gap.
- 12 new look-alike cars (see `docs/car-audit.md`).
- Known: Joker lanes now cost +1 to +11 s for the AI (Bauxite is the highest), above the spec range, by design.

## Sixth pass: sky, car fittings, debug panel

- New papercraft sky: three drifting cloud decks, stepped paper tones, silver-lined edges, light shafts from sun and moon, sunset glow band, paper moon, twinkling stars, and a dark layered storm deck with rain curtains.
- Grille shapes per style, roof racks and bull bars on utility bodies.
- Settings > Graphics > Debug panel: car, track, sector, lap, surface, position, speed and world state on the race screen.

## Seventh pass

- Apex cones are real objects now: driving through one knocks it over and sends it tumbling.
- Night circuits get lamp masts and light pools along the main straight.
- A broadcast helicopter flies the course on a schedule (first pass about 22 s in, then every ~110 s), with rotor sound and a night searchlight.
- New loading screen: cut-paper sunrise or sunset, large stage name, preview card and tips.

## Eighth pass

- Start lights are housings with the lens on the face toward the grid (they used to glow on every side).
- Finish-line confetti cannons and fireworks fire when the first car (and again when the player) finishes a circuit race.
- Circuit grids are spaced 12.5 m between rows (was 8 m), wider lanes, and staggered by half a row.

## Ninth pass

- Tyre barriers are now stacks of three painted tyres (red and white in turn) with tread rings and dark centres.
- Light glow is on by default (Settings > Graphics > Light glow). Vehicle lamps and floodlights use a round glow; street and track lamps throw light downward only (small halo at the head, a faint cone, a pool on the ground).

## Tenth pass

- Subtle light glow added to lit house windows (dusk and night), the control tower windows, marshal booth flag panels and the start lights (when lit).
- Co-driver voice pack for Nick replaced with the new recording (137 calls, converted to mono 24 kHz Ogg matched to -18.5 LUFS like the other packs).

## Eleventh pass

- Hidden lumps: bank slope was stepped between samples (now interpolated), the road-height station follows the car smoothly through hairpins (it used to stall and jump), the elevation profile is smoothed, the lap line no longer carries a 0.1 m step, and the launch margin is wider so steep grades do not throw the car into the air. The all-circuit bench (`--rx --rxbounce`) now shows launches only at the built jumps.
- Street and track lamps light cars passing beneath them (a warm light follows the car near a lamp).
- Car glass reflects the sky (and a faint glint of lamps at night).
- Smashed lamps stay dark: beam, glow halo, tail lamp and the lit lens panes go out when the crushed patch reaches them.
- Tyre walls are real rings with a hole through the stack (no cap on top).
- Loading screen and splash use a rendered sunset hero image (bloom, depth of field, backlit car).

## Twelfth pass

- Wet-road lamp reflections on rain stages at dusk and night (track and street lamps mirror in the road).
- Soft contact shadows in front of tyre walls, concrete and hay, and a stronger softer shadow under cars.
- Sponsor boards hang on long straight runs of tyre wall, concrete, Armco and fence (fictional sponsors, at most 70 per stage).
- Procedural ground detail (`shaders/surface.gdshaderinc`): tarmac aggregate, cracks and patches; fine and coarse gravel pebbles; sand ripples; packed dirt and clay; churned mud with puddles; cracked ice; snow drifts with glitter. Used for rallycross sectors, the Joker lane, rally gravel/mud/ice/snow patches and the base road. Off with "Paper fibre & grain detail" off.

## Thirteenth pass

- Night lights in the sunset/sunrise journey: track lamps, floodlight glow and window glow now follow the fading light (they only existed for fixed dusk/night before), headlights are brighter at night, and wet-road reflections follow the same level.
- Tyre walls: a hard hit (over about 4 m/s into the wall) knocks one to three tyres off. They tumble onto the road, settle, and are kicked on by passing cars (at most 18 loose tyres).

## Fourteenth pass

- The procedural per-pixel ground shader (pebbles, cracks, ripples) was removed: it cost too much on phones (45 fps on a Galaxy S26 Ultra at High). Roads and sectors use the original paper material again.
- Surface character now comes from plain geometry (no per-pixel cost): loose berms along the shoulders of gravel, dirt, sand and mud sectors, scattered stones and clods, dark mud puddles, and on tarmac repair patches and cracks.

## Fifteenth pass

- Joker island: a divider runs the whole length of the gap between road and lane (a single mid-gap wall where the void is too narrow for two). The AI crosses to the lane earlier so it still takes it (Chalk Valley checked: taken, about +3.6 s).
- Tyre-wall chicanes on Chalk Valley, Timberline, Breton, Baltic, Apex and Harbor Grid (solid, AI weaves).
- Truck, SUV and buggy engines are louder; the truck also gets a sub-octave layer.
- Sponsor boards fixed (card was sitting half a board too high, so the text fell off the bottom); tarmac repair patches much subtler; knocked-off tyres are thrown further onto the road (up to four).

## Sixteenth pass

- Snow and mud: snowbanks (mud banks on wet stages) along road edges on rally stages; deep-rut lips beside both wheel tracks on mud and snow sectors; circuit grooves darken at laps 2 and 4; mud and snow relief on rally patches; mud splatter builds on the car flanks.
- Speed: reflector posts every ~20 m near the road edge on rally stages; the camera widens its view with speed and trembles slightly (Settings > "Speed effects").
- Ambient: dust/pollen motes (flurries on snowy stages), waving flags around circuits, cheering crowds near the action.
- Presentation: a 6 s skippable intro flyover round the start, and a slow orbit of the car after the finish.

## Seventeenth pass

- First visit to Circuits shows an animated Joker explainer (what it is, take it once, it costs time, 15 s penalty if skipped). Replay from Settings.
- Joker penalty raised from 10 s to 15 s. The measured AI Joker cost is about 2-10 s (worst: Breton), so skipping always costs more than taking it.

## Eighteenth pass

- Joker explainer rebuilt on a real in-game overhead image of Chalk Valley's Joker lane with animated gold lane, flowing chevrons, a moving car and the on-screen tag; same five steps and 15 s penalty message.
- Sponsor arches: black tyre-band arches with yellow trim and the sponsor name span the road three times per stage, on straight clear stretches (circuits and rally stages). A drivable humpback bridge was not built.

## Nineteenth pass

- Quality tiers (Settings.fx_level: Low/Medium/High): sky layers 1/2/3 and light shafts, glow sprites, banks, markers, arches, motes, car rim light and distance haze need Medium+; wet-road reflections, surface relief, ruts and radial speed blur need High.
- Camera: sits lower and further back as speed rises, and rolls slightly into corners.
- Radial speed blur at the screen edges (High only).
- Low sun: softer, longer, stronger shadows. Cars get a thin sky-tinted rim light. Distance haze, thicker at dawn.
- Gravel spray is richer; sparks fly when a car scrapes Armco, concrete or fence. Exhaust flames not done.

## Twentieth pass

- Level of detail: pines, trees, birches and bushes switch to a two-card far form beyond about 24-42 m (a pine was about 5,700 triangles close up). Forest stage triangles: 1.0M to about 0.4M on High, 0.74M to about 0.09M on Medium (desktop render).
- Dynamic resolution (Settings > Automatic resolution) lowers the render scale when frames run long and raises it with headroom, never above the player's chosen scale.
- First-launch self-check (fresh installs only): hidden benchmark behind the title picks High, Medium or Low.
- Effects: car-tinted dust; stones that land and bounce; heat shimmer (dry day, High); rain beads on the lens in bonnet/cockpit views and a wetter rim on cars in rain; lightning and thunder on storm stages; flags that react to passing cars; crowds jump after jumps/splashes; night photographers with flashes; lit sponsor boards, car underglow and a bigger start-light haze at night; skid marks last longer; cinematic three-shot camera after the finish.
- Not done: a true rewind replay, exhaust heat haze, dynamic drying of water trails.

## Twenty-first pass

- Plants sway in the wind (vertex shader, strength grows with height; stronger in rain). Houses and cars get a darkened silhouette edge (Medium+).
- LOD now also covers rocks, logs and houses/barns/cabins; far trees get per-tree colour variation. Seasons: Settings > Season (stage default, green, autumn).
- Rainbow on daytime rain stages after about 40 s; sunset light shafts (High); puddle ripples; window glow flicker; wetter road sheen.
- Cars: dust film in dry loose conditions (mud in wet), brake discs glow after hard braking (visible at night), parts fly off in hard hits, cracked windscreen overlay in bonnet/cockpit views.
- Circuits: chequered start line, grid boxes, centre dashes and arrows on tarmac. Rally stages: crumbling edge chips and grass tufts on the verge (High).
- Camera: slight FOV squeeze when braking hard, a small kick on gear changes. Lap delta vs best lap at each quarter-lap gate (circuits).
- Texture compression was already on (ETC2/ASTC import); scenery batching left as is (draw calls measured at 130-280).

## Twenty-second pass: brake discs and the deferred visuals

- Wheels now have open spokes and gaps so the vented disc and red caliper behind them show (inspected in a lineup render only).
- Front/rear bumpers and both mirrors are separate meshes; after 3+ hard hits (impact strength > 9) the nearest one disappears and a matching piece is thrown. Not exercised in a real collision test.
- Disc heat shimmer: a small post-process wobble over each of the player's wheels after hard braking (not the exhaust). Untested on device.
- Trees shed leaves (or snow clumps on snow/alpine stages) when a car above 12 m/s passes within 5.5 m; pooled, 6 s cooldown per tree. Not visually verified.
- Cockpit: gloved hands at 10 and 2 o'clock turning with the wheel (render-checked).
- Sunrise stages get six stacked low mist banks instead of three. Not visually compared.
- Fence, armco and wall chunks drop out beyond 240-480 m depending on quality tier.

## Twenty-third pass: disc glow tuning, results-screen idle, QA

- Disc heat builds ~2x faster and decays at 0.085/s (was 0.18/s): a hard stop glows for roughly 8-12 s instead of ~5 s. Daylight strength 0.35 -> 0.85, glow colour x2, plus a soft bloom sprite per wheel.
- Results screen: 18 s after the finish the 3D world behind the results page is frozen, frame rate capped at 20, 3D scale at most 0.75 and dynamic resolution paused; restored when leaving the page.
- Dev: `--bench --soak [--teleport --manualcam --grid --soakfor=N --soakevery=N --soakshots]` leaves a finished race running and logs node/object/memory growth; `--heat`, `--sidecam` for brake-disc close-ups.
- The reported crash could NOT be reproduced in the soak (node count, objects and memory flat for 10 minutes of results time); the freeze above is a mitigation, not a confirmed root-cause fix.

## Twenty-fourth pass: career ladder, menu fixes, podium results

- Championships are now a three-tier ladder: Rookie Cup (5 x 4 km), Pro Series (9 rounds: 5 x 8 km stages + 4 rallycross), Champion's Trial (13 rounds: 15 km stages, twilight Taiga + 8 rallycross). Finishing a tier unlocks the next. Old cup records for the removed series are no longer shown.
- Carousel swipe is read at viewport level, so it works over the cards (the championship cards fill the strip, which is why only the circuit carousel responded before). A swipe suppresses the tap on release.
- AI difficulty and opponents moved from Settings to the Race and Championship confirmation screens (hidden for Time Trial). Opponents lock after round one of a championship.
- New `RaceResults` screen (scripts/ui/race_results.gd): paper podium, rally stamp, classification with gap bars, championship points and rank movement. Animation is finite (~4.5 s), tap to skip, redraw loop stops afterwards.
- Dev views: `--view=raceresults|champresults [--pos=N --final]`, `--view=champintro`.

## Twenty-fifth pass: car classes

- Four racing classes (Catalog.CAR_CLASS): Heritage (8), Groups (11), Elite (8), Off-road (4). Shown on the car-select card.
- "Cars: Any class / <Class> only" option on the Race and Championship confirmation screens (setting `same_class`, off by default). Same-class grids use each rival in turn and repeat models in an alternate livery when the class is smaller than the grid (Off-road with 5 opponents repeats Badger and Mule). Locked after round one of a championship.
- Dev: `--car=N --sameclass --gridinfo` print the grid.
